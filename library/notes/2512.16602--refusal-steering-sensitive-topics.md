## Abstract

Paraphrased source note: the paper proposes inference-time refusal steering for
sensitive topics, using judge-based refusal confidence and ridge-regularized
steering vectors. It reports Qwen3-family results, deeper-layer signal
concentration, and distributed refusal dimensions.
