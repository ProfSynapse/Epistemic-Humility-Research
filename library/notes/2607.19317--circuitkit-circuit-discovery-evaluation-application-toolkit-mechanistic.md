## Abstract

Circuit analysis can support not only model explanation but also downstream interventions such as pruning, editing, steering, and selective fine-tuning. However, conducting such analyses currently requires stitching together separate implementations for discovery, evaluation, and intervention, as well as hand-authoring the contrastive prompts required by many discovery methods. This fragmentation makes methods difficult to compare and limits their application beyond canonical tasks. We introduce CircuitKIT, a source-available library that connects the circuit-analysis workflow through a typed, serializable representation. CircuitKIT provides a suite of discovery algorithms, declarative interfaces for mapping structured data into discovery tasks, complementary circuit diagnostics, and downstream application modules. Together, these components provide common infrastructure for conducting and comparing circuit analyses. The library, examples, notebooks, and documentation are released at https://github.com/Lexsi-Labs/CircuitKIT .

## Summary

CircuitKIT is a source-available systems library for carrying a circuit hypothesis from discovery through diagnostic evaluation, intervention, export, and standardized benchmarking. Its central architectural choice is the typed, serializable CircuitScores artifact, which records the model, task, algorithm, granularity, configuration, and per-component scores. The stateful pipeline, flat API, and YAML CLI all produce and consume this same artifact, so discovery backends and application modules can be varied independently (Sections 3 and 10; Figures 1 and 5).

The discovery layer packages thirteen algorithms across four backend families, with six marked stable across GPT-2, Llama, Gemma, and Qwen: EAP, EAP-IG, EAP-GP, ACDC, IBCircuit, and CD-T (Section 4; Table 2). CircuitKIT also separates contrastive methods from clean-only methods and supplies a template-driven path from CSV, JSONL, or HuggingFace rows to aligned discovery tasks. The data preflight checks token alignment, answer determinism, signal, balance, uniqueness, and corruption consistency before discovery compute is spent (Section 5).

The evaluation contract is unusually relevant to this research program. Six core pillars report soft causal patching, hard-ablation sufficiency, resample stability, corruption robustness, matched baselines, and cross-task generalization separately. An optional seventh pillar defines cross-seed intervention reliability. This structure matters because the empirical studies show that behavioral recovery, component overlap, hard-ablation sufficiency, and downstream intervention performance can disagree (Sections 6 and 11).

The application layer covers structural pruning, mixed-precision quantization, selective fine-tuning, knowledge editing, activation steering, circuit-restricted LoRA healing, and hallucination probing, with exported checkpoints scored through lm-evaluation-harness (Sections 7 to 9). The paper does not claim that circuit guidance always beats conventional baselines. Its intervention studies instead show that the value of a circuit ranking depends on the operator that consumes it, and that downstream effects must be measured directly.

## Extracted numbers

- **Toolkit coverage (Table 1; Sections 3 to 7):** CircuitKIT exposes 13 discovery algorithms, of which 6 are stable-tier, 6 core evaluation diagnostics, 1 optional intervention-reliability pillar, and 7 application modules behind the shared CircuitScores artifact.
- **IOI algorithm comparison (Section 11.1; Table 3; Figure 6):** On GPT-2 Small at node level, EAP, EAP-IG, and EAP-GP each reach patching faithfulness 1.000 and canonical-head recall 0.600 to 0.667. CD-T reaches patching 0.894 and ablation 1.000 but canonical-head recall 0.000, showing that behavioral recovery and canonical component overlap disagree.
- **Cross-family discovery (Section 11.2; Table 4):** EAP-IG completes all 12 model-task cells across GPT-2 Small, Pythia-1.4B, Llama-3.2-1B, Gemma-2-2B, Qwen2.5-1.5B, and Phi-2. Patching recovery is at least 0.914 and three-seed Jaccard stability ranges from 0.801 to 0.919, while hard-ablation sufficiency ranges from 0.244 to 1.000.
- **Neuron-level discovery (Section 11.3; Table 5):** On Llama-3.2-1B IOI, neuron-level EAP-IG retains patching faithfulness 1.000 with ablation faithfulness 0.241. Single-point EAP reaches patching 0.893 but hard-ablation faithfulness -0.350, a behavioral inversion that a clipped score would hide.
- **Custom-data refusal path (Section 11.4; Table 6; Figure 7):** The alignment filter retains 265 of 334 paired rows and drops 69 misaligned pairs. The EAP-IG circuit recovers 0.854 of the refusal gap and beats random and magnitude subgraphs by 1.71x and 1.44x, yet hard isolation yields signed ablation -2.61 with raw refusal logit difference -1.55. The clean-only IBCircuit route reaches circuit-only P(correct) 0.902 versus 0.874 for the full model.
- **Structural pruning (Section 11.5; Table 7; Figure 8):** At 30 percent sparsity on Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct BoolQ, IBCircuit retains 98.6 percent of base accuracy, while EAP-IG and CD-T retain 57.1 and 50.2 percent. Across ten selectors in this cell, patch faithfulness anti-correlates with accuracy retention at Spearman rho -0.78, p=0.010.
- **Mixed-precision quantization (Section 11.6; Table 8):** Patch faithfulness does not significantly predict accuracy retention across nine selector means, rho +0.23, p=0.55, while ablation faithfulness does, rho +0.73, p=0.031.
- **Selective fine-tuning (Section 11.7; Table 9):** Across the broader 16 selector-model cells, mean accuracy difference between circuit and random equal-budget masks is -0.001. Both 30 percent mask types keep WikiText-2 perplexity near the unmodified model, while full-parameter training degrades it, supporting the frozen budget rather than circuit identity as the coherence guard.

## Relevance to experiment

CircuitKIT is a strong architectural reference for Synaptic Tuner, but its most valuable contribution is the contract rather than an immediate dependency. Synaptic Tuner should preserve a typed, serializable circuit record that includes model revision, task and contrast construction, hook coordinates, granularity, scoring method, selected components, normalization, seed, and provenance. Discovery recipes should emit that record, while causal evaluations and write interventions consume it without assuming which reader produced it.

The six-pillar evaluation design should inform a reusable circuit-audit recipe for this research repository. At minimum, an epistemic-humility circuit should be scored under soft patching and hard isolation, compared with size- and layer-matched random controls, re-discovered across data resamples, tested under answer-preserving and answer-changing perturbations, and evaluated on a held-out task or dataset. Signed outcomes should be retained so that refusal or abstention inversions are visible rather than clipped away.

The paper also argues against promoting intrinsic faithfulness into an actionability claim. For any pruning, steering, editing, or selective-training cell, the downstream behavioral effect, general capability, fluency, and cross-seed dispersion should be measured directly. The paper's optional intervention-reliability statistic is a useful candidate for the experiment schema, but its own intervention studies are single-seed, so the metric should be treated as a proposed contract rather than validated evidence.

Adoption should start with an adapter and a capability audit, not a wholesale dependency. CircuitKIT v1.0.0 is pinned to TransformerLens 2.18 for discovery and evaluation, has a narrower architecture registry for weight interventions, and uses the non-OSI LSAL v1.1 license. Any integration should therefore keep Synaptic Tuner's generic hook and recipe abstractions independent, map them to CircuitScores at a boundary, and separately review whether the license permits the intended distribution and use.

## Claims

- CircuitKIT connects thirteen discovery algorithms, a six-pillar diagnostic panel, and seven downstream modules through one typed CircuitScores artifact, allowing the three stages to vary without method-specific format conversion. (Sections 3 to 10; Figures 1 and 5) [[shared-circuit-artifact-decouples-discovery-evaluation-intervention]]
- Behavioral recovery is not equivalent to canonical component recovery: CD-T reaches patching 0.894 and ablation 1.000 on GPT-2 IOI while recovering none of the canonical heads in its top set. (Section 11.1; Table 3; Figure 6)
- A custom-data refusal circuit can appear faithful under soft patching yet invert toward compliance when isolated by hard ablation. (Section 11.4; Table 6; Figure 7) [[hard-ablation-can-invert-patch-faithful-refusal-circuits]]
- Circuit actionability is intervention-specific: patch faithfulness anti-correlates with pruning retention in the highlighted BoolQ cell, ablation faithfulness predicts quantization retention, and circuit masks do not beat random equal-budget masks in selective fine-tuning. (Sections 11.5 to 11.8; Tables 7 to 9; Figure 8) [[intervention-operator-mediates-circuit-actionability]]
- Freezing 70 percent of weights, rather than choosing the updated 30 percent by circuit importance, is what preserves WikiText-2 coherence in the reported selective fine-tuning study. (Section 11.7; Table 9) [[fixed-update-budget-protects-coherence-independent-of-circuit-selection]]
