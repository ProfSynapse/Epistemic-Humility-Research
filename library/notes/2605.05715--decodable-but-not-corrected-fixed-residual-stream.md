## Abstract

Can linearly decodable failure signals in LLM hidden states be leveraged to correct those failures? We investigate this classification-correction gap via Overthinking (OT)--a stable behavioral regime (Jaccard >= 0.81, 94% inter-annotator agreement) in medical QA where models answer correctly under resampling yet fail in extended chain-of-thought. OT is linearly decodable at 71.6% balanced accuracy (p < 10^{-16}). Yet five families of fixed linear steering (29 configurations, n=1,273) all yield Delta ~= 0, with identical null results cross-architecture (Qwen2.5-7B) and cross-domain (MMLU-STEM). Three convergent lines of evidence suggest representational entanglement: the OT direction has 85-88% overlap with task-critical computation (specificity ratio <= 0.152); non-targeted shared-direction steering damages accuracy (-12.1pp); and LEACE concept erasure damages accuracy (-3.6pp, p=0.01), while 10 random erasures produce Delta=+0.3pp. The per-instance probe-steering correlation is r=-0.002 (p=0.97). Positively, the same probe enables selective abstention (held-out AUROC=0.610, exceeding all five uncertainty baselines, p=0.009): decodable failure structure supports post-generation reliability estimation even when the fixed linear steering family cannot exploit it for correction.

## Claims

- Evidence label: experiment (Table 3, Section 4.3, Section 5.3, Abstract). Overthinking (OT) is linearly decodable at 71.6% accuracy (balanced accuracy 62.3%, AUROC 0.672, p~=10^-16), yet five families of fixed linear activation steering (29 configurations, n=1,273) on Llama-3.1-8B produce Delta ~= 0, replicated cross-architecture on Qwen2.5-7B (9 configs, Delta in [-0.9,+0.8]pp) and cross-domain on MMLU-STEM (n=300).
- Evidence label: experiment (Section 4.6 'Shared-Specific Decomposition', Table 3, Appendix H). The failure-mode direction is representationally entangled with task-relevant computation: specificity ratio is only 0.119 (Llama) / 0.152 (Qwen), and forcing correction via uniform shared-direction steering (-12.1pp) or LEACE-style concept erasure (-3.6pp, p=0.01) damages accuracy rather than fixing it, while 10 random-direction erasures of equal rank yield +0.3pp -- evidence the entanglement is causal, not merely correlational.
- Evidence label: experiment (Figure 4, Tables 6-7, Section 5.3/6 'Selective abstention'). A held-out correctness probe (layer 21) enables selective abstention with test AUROC=0.610, exceeding all five tested single-forward-pass uncertainty baselines (best baseline AUROC=0.569, Delta AUROC=0.041, p=0.009), even though the same probe-derived structure cannot be exploited by steering for correction.
- Evidence label: experiment (Table 4, Section 6 'Prompt-based baselines'). Ten prompt-based baselines (few-shot, no-CoT, self-refinement, verbalized confidence) also fail to correct OT: none reliably improves accuracy, and self-refinement/verbalized-confidence significantly degrade it (-5.9pp / -5.7pp, p<0.001), extending the classification-correction gap beyond activation steering to input-level interventions.
