## Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) have a wealth of knowledge that allows them to excel in various Natural Language Processing (NLP) tasks. Current research focuses on enhancing their performance within their existing knowledge. Despite their vast knowledge, LLMs are still limited by the amount of information they can accommodate and comprehend. Therefore, the ability to understand their own limitations on the unknows, referred to as self-knowledge, is of paramount importance. This study aims to evaluate LLMs' self-knowledge by assessing their ability to identify unanswerable or unknowable questions. We introduce an automated methodology to detect uncertainty in the responses of these models, providing a novel measure of their self-knowledge. We further introduce a unique dataset, SelfAware, consisting of unanswerable questions from five diverse categories and their answerable counterparts. Our extensive analysis, involving 20 LLMs including GPT-3, InstructGPT, and LLaMA, discovering an intrinsic capacity for self-knowledge within these models. Moreover, we demonstrate that in-context learning and instruction tuning can further enhance this self-knowledge. Despite this promising insight, our findings also highlight a considerable gap between the capabilities of these models and human proficiency in recognizing the limits of their knowledge.

## Summary

Yin et al. introduce SelfAware, a benchmark of 1,032 genuinely unanswerable questions across five categories (No Scientific Consensus 25%, Completely Subjective 27%, Philosophical 23%, Imagination 15%, Too Many Variables 10%), paired with 2,337 answerable counterparts drawn from SQuAD (1,487), HotpotQA (182), and TriviaQA (668) via SimCSE-guided semantic matching. The paper simultaneously introduces an automated uncertainty detector (also SimCSE-based) and the Self-Knowledge F1 metric, which treats unanswerable questions as the positive class and measures whether model responses contain language similar to 16 hand-curated uncertain-meaning reference sentences at cosine similarity threshold 0.75. Twenty LLMs are evaluated across three prompt formats (direct, instruction, ICL). Three levers consistently raise self-knowledge F1: (1) model scale, monotonically within GPT families, consistent with scaling-law expectations; (2) instruction tuning, with InstructGPT models exceeding GPT-3 counterparts and Vicuna-13B exceeding LLaMA-65B; (3) ICL, which yields a 27.96% gain for davinci over the direct prompt and also narrows the gap between base and instruction-tuned models, suggesting latent self-knowledge representations are present but require a prompt cue to surface. GPT-4 achieves the highest model F1 at 75.47%, while two human volunteers scored 84.37% and 85.48% (average 84.93%) on the same 100-question sample, a 9.46-point gap that frames the benchmark's headline challenge. The paper's evaluation protocol is fully specified in the appendix (reference sentence list, threshold ablation, templates), making it straightforward to rerun against new checkpoints.

- GPT-4 Self-Knowledge F1: 75.47% on a random 100-question SelfAware sample (instruction input form), the highest among 20 tested LLMs (Section 4.4, Figure 3).
- GPT-4-to-human self-knowledge gap: 84.93% minus 75.47% = 9.46 percentage points (derived from Section 4.3 and Section 4.4).
- ICL input form yields a 27.96% Self-Knowledge F1 improvement over the direct input form for the davinci model (Section 4.4 Input Forms, Figures 2 and 4).
- SimCSE similarity threshold ablation: threshold 0.75 yields the highest F1 of 91.89 on 100 text-davinci-002 responses, balancing precision 100.00% and recall 85.00% (Table 2 / Appendix A.2).
- Reference sentence set: 16 sentences with uncertain meanings, manually curated from GPT-3 and InstructGPT outputs (Appendix A.1).
- Closed-book QA accuracy on answerable subset, InstructGPT series: text-ada-001 2.48%, text-davinci-001 10.61%, GPT-4 42.64% (Section 4.4 Answerable Questions, Figure 6).

## Relevance to experiment

SelfAware is a primary out-of-domain evaluation target in the locked training-regimen SFT-vs-DPO-vs-KTO abstention study (it is the dataset on which R-Tuning and related methods are tested). The paper establishes three levers for self-knowledge improvement (scale, instruction tuning, ICL) that frame what any new training arm must be compared against: ICL alone recovers nearly as much as instruction tuning for some model families, setting a prompt-only reference point. The SimCSE-based evaluation protocol is fully specified and cheap to rerun, making it a candidate automated evaluation for new checkpoints during the experiment. The 9.46-point GPT-4-to-human gap confirms that even the strongest available model at the time of writing falls short of human self-knowledge, which anchors the research motivation. The paper does not report a training-intervention effect size in the effects.csv sense; it is a diagnostic benchmark paper whose numbers populate the baseline row rather than an intervention cell.

## Claims

- GPT-4 achieves a self-knowledge F1 of 75.47%, compared to a human benchmark of 84.93%, indicating a substantial gap between the best LLM and human self-knowledge. (Figure 3, Section 4.4) [[self-knowledge-f1]]
- Instruction tuning reliably improves self-knowledge: InstructGPT models outperform their GPT-3 base counterparts, and Vicuna-13B outperforms LLaMA-65B on the SelfAware benchmark. (Figure 2, Figure 5, Section 4.4) [[instruction-tuning]]
- In-context learning (ICL) yields a 27.96% self-knowledge F1 improvement over the direct input form for the davinci model, and reduces the performance gap between base and instruction-tuned models. (Section 4.4, Figure 2, Figure 4) [[in-context-learning]]
- The SelfAware dataset contains 1,032 unanswerable questions across five categories and 2,337 answerable counterparts, providing a more diverse benchmark than prior unanswerable-question datasets like SQuAD2.0. (Section 2, Table 1) [[selfaware]]
