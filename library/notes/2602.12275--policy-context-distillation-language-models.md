## Abstract

Context distillation enables language models to internalize in-context knowledge into their parameters. In our work, we propose On-Policy Context Distillation (OPCD), a framework that bridges on-policy distillation with context distillation by training a student model on its own generated trajectories while minimizing reverse Kullback-Leibler divergence against a context-conditioned teacher. We demonstrate the effectiveness of OPCD on two important applications: experiential knowledge distillation, where models extract and consolidate transferable knowledge from their historical solution traces, and system prompt distillation, where models internalize beneficial behaviors encoded in optimized prompts. Across mathematical reasoning, text-based games, and domain-specific tasks, OPCD consistently outperforms baseline methods, achieving higher task accuracy while better preserving out-of-distribution capabilities. We further show that OPCD enables effective cross-size distillation, where smaller student models can internalize experiential knowledge from larger teachers.

## Summary

The paper introduces On-Policy Context Distillation, which trains a
context-free student on its own trajectories while a context-conditioned
teacher supplies token distributions. Reverse KL aligns the student's
generation distribution to the teacher and transfers the context into the
student's parameters.

The authors test two applications. Experiential knowledge distillation extracts
and accumulates lessons from prior model solution traces. System-prompt
distillation transfers optimized medical and safety instructions into weights.
OPCD usually improves accuracy over off-policy context distillation and better
preserves out-of-distribution capabilities.

## Extracted numbers

- For Qwen3-8B math, test-time experiential knowledge with OPCD scored 79.7
  versus 78.5 for off-policy context distillation and 75.0 for the base model
  (Table 1).
- For filtered Frozen Lake experience, OPCD scored 38.3 versus 35.2 for context
  distillation and 6.3 for the base model (Table 2).
- On the medical system-prompt task, OPCD scored 76.7 for Llama-3.1-8B,
  76.3 for Llama-3.2-3B, and 62.3 for Qwen2.5-7B (Table 3).
- On the safety task, OPCD scored 79.6 for Llama-3.1-8B, 83.1 for
  Llama-3.2-3B, and 78.1 for Qwen2.5-7B (Table 4).
- Frozen teacher-student OPCD scored 53.9 versus 18.8 for self-distillation on
  Sokoban (Table 5).

## Relevance to experiment

OPCD directly targets weight-level internalization. It removes the source
context at student inference and trains the model to match a teacher that still
sees that context. The system-prompt experiments show that optimized behavioral
instructions can be compressed into parameters, while the experiential tests
show the same pattern for learned task knowledge. The paper does not condition
weight updates on an internal answerability readout.

## Claims

- Evidence label: method. OPCD samples context-free student trajectories and
  minimizes token-level reverse KL to a context-conditioned teacher
  (Section 3, Algorithm 1).
- Evidence label: comparative benchmark. OPCD usually exceeded off-policy
  context distillation on math, text games, medicine, and safety tasks
  (Section 4.3, Tables 1 to 4).
- Evidence label: retention evaluation. On-policy training preserved IF-Eval
  and cross-domain medical performance better than the off-policy baseline
  (Section 4.5, Tables 1 and 2, Figure 3).
- Evidence label: configuration ablation. A frozen teacher was more stable and
  more accurate than simultaneous self-distillation on Sokoban and medicine
  (Section 4.6, Table 5).
