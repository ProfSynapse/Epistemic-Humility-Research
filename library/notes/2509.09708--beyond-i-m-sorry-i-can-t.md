## Abstract

Refusal on harmful prompts is a key safety behaviour in instruction-tuned large language models (LLMs), yet the internal causes of this behaviour remain poorly understood. We study two public instruction-tuned models, Gemma-2-2B-IT and LLaMA-3.1-8B-IT, using sparse autoencoders (SAEs) trained on residual-stream activations. Given a harmful prompt, we search the SAE latent space for feature sets whose ablation flips the model from refusal to compliance, demonstrating causal influence and creating a jailbreak. Our search proceeds in three stages: (1) Refusal Direction: find a refusal-mediating direction and collect SAE features near that direction; (2) Greedy Filtering: prune to a minimal set; and (3) Interaction Discovery: fit a factorization machine (FM) that captures nonlinear interactions among the remaining active features and the minimal set. This pipeline yields a broad set of jailbreak-critical features, offering insight into the mechanistic basis of refusal. Moreover, we find evidence of redundant features that remain dormant unless earlier features are suppressed. Our findings highlight the potential for fine-grained auditing and targeted intervention in safety behaviours by manipulating the interpretable latent space.

## Claims

- After Stage 2 greedy pruning, ablating 110 (LLaMA) or 2538 (Gemma) SAE features raises ASR from ~8% (71/861) to 0.70 for LLaMA and from ~0.5% (4/861) to 0.33 for Gemma, demonstrating these features are causally sufficient for refusal. (Table 1) [[sae-causal-feature-discovery]]
- Dormant SAE features (77 for LLaMA, 1656 for Gemma) that show zero activation on harmful prompts are nonetheless causally necessary: removing them from the minimal critical set causes jailbreaks to fail, providing direct evidence of the nonlinear hydra effect. (Stage 3 analysis, Figure 2) [[refusal-hydra-effect]]
- FM-based interaction discovery jailbreaks 372 samples on LLaMA compared to only 101 from an equally sized linear-probe feature set, demonstrating that refusal-critical features interact nonlinearly and cannot be adequately identified by linear methods. (Factorization Machines vs Linear Probe analysis section) [[factorization-machine]]
- 74% of redundant dormant features in LLaMA become active on system tokens (97% on <|begin_of_text|>) after ablating the primary active set, and clamping these features on benign prompts induces harm-related or refusal-like outputs, confirming their causal role in safety behavior. (Redundant Features analysis section) [[refusal-hydra-effect]]
