## Abstract

Paraphrased source note: the paper argues that the single-direction account of
LLM refusal is incomplete. It studies multiple categories of refusal and
non-compliance and reports that they occupy distinct directions, even though
linear steering along refusal-related directions can produce similar refusal to
over-refusal tradeoffs.

## Claims

- Evidence label: mechanism caveat. Refusal-like behaviors can map to distinct
  directions rather than one universal direction.
- Evidence label: steering caveat. Similar refusal/over-refusal tradeoffs can
  appear even when the underlying refusal categories differ.
