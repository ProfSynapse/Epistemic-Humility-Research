## Abstract

Steering vectors work for some concepts and layers but fail for others, and practitioners have no way to predict which setting applies before running an intervention. We introduce the Linear Accessibility Profile (LAP), a per-layer diagnostic that repurposes the logit lens as a predictor of steering vector effectiveness. The key measure, $A_{\mathrm{lin}}$, applies the model&#39;s unembedding matrix to intermediate hidden states, requiring no training. Across 24 controlled binary concept families on five models (Pythia-2.8B to Llama-8B), peak $A_{\mathrm{lin}}$ predicts steering effectiveness at $\rho = +0.86$ to $+0.91$ and layer selection at $\rho = +0.63$ to $+0.92$. A three-regime framework explains when difference-of-means steering works, when nonlinear methods are needed, and when no method can work. An entity-steering demo confirms the prediction end-to-end: steering at the LAP-recommended layer redirects completions on Gemma-2-2B and OLMo-2-1B-Instruct, while the middle layer (the standard heuristic) has no effect on either model.

## Claims

- Evidence label: experiment. Peak A_lin (logit-lens accuracy) predicts maximum
  steering Delta P at rho=+0.86 to +0.91 across 24 controlled binary concept
  families on five models (Pythia-2.8B, Gemma-2-2B, Qwen2.5-7B, Llama-3.1-8B,
  Qwen2.5-1.5B), all p<10^-3; restricted to families above the A_lin=0 floor,
  the correlation strengthens on larger models (rho=+0.86 on Qwen-7B, +0.84 on
  Llama-8B) (Figure 2, Section 4.2, Table 15-16).
- Evidence label: experiment. The standard 'steer at the middle layer' heuristic
  fails for factual concepts on Gemma-2-2B because the middle layer (L15) sits
  in regime 2 of the three-regime framework: all five core concept families
  show A_mlp>0.7 but A_lin=0 there, so difference-of-means steering has no
  effect; steering only works at late layers (L22-25) where A_lin>0 (Table 2,
  Figure 1, Section 4.2).
- Evidence label: experiment. A trained logistic-regression probe achieves
  >93% classification accuracy at every layer L0-L25 for all five core concept
  families on Gemma-2-2B, yet steering at the probe's best-accuracy layer
  produces near-zero effect (e.g. sequence: Delta P=+0.004 vs. oracle
  Delta P=+0.350), showing that generic linear separability in the
  high-dimensional residual stream does not predict steering success -- only
  alignment with the model's own unembedding readout (A_lin) does (Appendix J,
  Table 11).
- Evidence label: experiment. An end-to-end entity-steering demo causally
  validates the prediction: steering London-answer prompts toward 'Paris' at
  the LAP-recommended layer redirects completions on both Gemma-2-2B (L22,
  rho=+0.663, p<0.001) and OLMo-2-1B-Instruct (L13, rho=+0.753, p<0.001),
  while steering at the standard middle-layer heuristic (L13 on Gemma, L8 on
  OLMo) has no effect on either model (Section 4.3, Appendix Q).
