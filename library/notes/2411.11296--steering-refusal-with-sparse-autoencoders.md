## Abstract

Paraphrased source note: the paper studies inference-time refusal steering by
amplifying sparse-autoencoder features associated with refusal. It reports
improved jailbreak robustness but also broad benchmark degradation, suggesting
that refusal-related features may be entangled with general capabilities.

## Claims

- Evidence label: intervention. SAE feature steering can alter refusal-related
  behavior.
- Evidence label: tradeoff. Refusal steering through SAE features can degrade
  unrelated benchmark performance.
