## Abstract

We observe an empirical phenomenon in Large Language Models (LLMs) -- very few activations exhibit significantly larger values than others (e.g., 100,000 times larger). We call them massive activations. First, we demonstrate the widespread existence of massive activations across various LLMs and characterize their locations. Second, we find their values largely stay constant regardless of the input, and they function as indispensable bias terms in LLMs. Third, these massive activations lead to the concentration of attention probabilities to their corresponding tokens, and further, implicit bias terms in the self-attention output. Last, we also study massive activations in Vision Transformers. Code is available at https://github.com/locuslab/massive-activations.

## Summary

Sun et al. discover massive activations: extremely rare scalar values in the residual stream (fewer than ~10 among tens of millions) whose magnitude exceeds ~100 and is ~1000x+ the hidden-state median. They occupy a couple of fixed feature dimensions (e.g. 1415 and 2533 in LLaMA2-7B) at special token positions (start token, first delimiter). Values stay near-constant across inputs and act as fixed implicit bias terms; they cause attention sinks and impose an implicit attention bias. Setting them to their mean is harmless, zeroing them collapses the model.

## Claims

- Evidence label: empirical. Massive activations exceed magnitude ~100 and are ~1000x+ the hidden-state median (LLaMA2-7B largest ~2000-2600 vs median ~0.2), numbering fewer than ~10 among tens of millions (Table 1; Fig 1; Sec 2). [[massive-activations]]
- Evidence label: empirical. They occupy a couple of fixed feature dimensions (LLaMA2-7B dims 1415 & 2533) at the start token and first delimiter (".", "\n"), emerging abruptly around layer 2 and persisting through middle layers (Sec 2.1-2.2; Fig 4). [[massive-activations]]
- Evidence label: intervention. Values are near-constant across inputs and act as fixed bias terms: setting them to their mean leaves perplexity/accuracy unchanged, zeroing them collapses the model (C4→inf, accuracy 68.95%→36.75% for LLaMA2-7B) (Table 3; Sec 3). [[massive-activations-act-as-fixed-attention-bias]]
- Evidence label: mechanistic. They cause attention sinks by concentrating attention on their tokens, whose near-identical value vectors inject a constant implicit attention bias; adding an explicit learnable attention bias eliminates them (Sec 4; Eq 2-3; Figs 5-8). [[attention-sink]] [[implicit-attention-bias]]
