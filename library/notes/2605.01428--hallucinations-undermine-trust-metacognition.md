## Abstract

This ICML 2026 Position Track paper argues that frontier language models still hallucinate in factoid question answering and that many factuality gains expand the model's knowledge boundary without making the model more aware of where that boundary lies. The authors frame hallucinations as confident errors and argue that eliminating hallucinations while preserving utility may face a tradeoff if the only choices are answering or abstaining. They propose a third path: faithful uncertainty, where a model's linguistic uncertainty aligns with its intrinsic uncertainty. On this view, metacognition is needed both for direct user interaction and for agentic control over when to search and what information to trust.

## Summary

The paper is useful as conceptual framing rather than as an intervention result. It connects hallucination, trust, and metacognition by treating the core failure as a mismatch between what the model internally should be uncertain about and how confidently it speaks. Its most relevant move for this project is the shift from "answer versus abstain" to "answer, abstain, or express calibrated uncertainty," which matches the synthesis's coherence/faithfulness axis.

The paper also sharpens the agentic-control version of the problem: uncertainty is not only a user-facing style choice, but a signal that should govern when a system searches, defers, or trusts retrieved information. That makes it a useful bridge between faithful calibration, abstention, and future tool-use/search policies.

The appendix simulation is useful only as conceptual scaffolding: it fixes a base hallucination rate of 25%, draws correct-answer confidence from Beta(1.8, 1.0), draws incorrect-answer confidence from Beta(1.0, 1.3), and uses isotonic regression to obtain smECE around 0.014 before illustrating utility-error tradeoffs under rejection thresholds. These are simulated assumptions, not experimental model results.

## Relevance to experiment

This supports the experiment program's focus on whether training changes a model's epistemic state or only its outward policy. For locked training-regimen it does not change the locked protocol, hypotheses, or run matrix. For later phases, it strengthens the rationale for measuring whether stated uncertainty, token-level confidence, hidden-state probes, and search/retrieval decisions move together rather than treating abstention alone as the endpoint.

## Claims

- Conceptual claim: Hallucination should be treated not only as incorrect generation but as confident error that undermines user trust.
- Design claim: A third response mode, faithful uncertainty, can preserve utility better than a binary answer-versus-abstain policy when the model is unsure.
- Agentic-control claim: Metacognition is relevant beyond user-facing hedging because uncertainty estimates should guide when an agent searches, defers, or trusts retrieved information.
