## Summary

Turner et al. introduce activation engineering as a paradigm and Activation Addition (ActAdd) as its concrete implementation. The core idea is that the residual stream of a decoder-only Transformer encodes high-level behavioral features as approximately linear directions, and that adding a scaled difference vector between two contrastive prompt activations to the residual stream at a chosen layer can steer generation without any weight update or backward pass. The steering vector is computed from as few as one contrast pair (two prompts), requiring no labeled dataset, no optimization, and no model modification.

The paper tests ActAdd on GPT-2-XL (1.5B) for perplexity and layer-sweep experiments, and on OPT-6.7B and LLaMA-3-8B for head-to-head toxicity and sentiment benchmarks. For the topic-steering layer sweep on GPT-2-XL, effectiveness rises until layer 6 (over 90% probability of wedding-related words, versus approximately 2% unsteered) and then declines, establishing a layer-optimal injection site for that vector. The perplexity experiments in the same section use l=16 for the same wedding vector, an internal inconsistency the paper does not explicitly resolve. For topic diversity, single-token topic vectors produce a 5-20% relevance boost across most topics at c=2, with "art" as an explicit exception that does not show the boost.

On RealToxicityPrompts (n=1000), ActAdd-OPT achieves toxicity 0.112 versus the next-best OPT-cohort method PREADD-D-OPT at 0.122, an 8% relative reduction. ActAdd-LLaMA3 lowers toxicity from 0.114 to 0.108, a modest 5% drop with no LLaMA3-specific competing method in the comparison table, so the SOTA language applies cleanly only within the OPT cohort. On the Stanford IMDb sentiment benchmark, ActAdd-LLaMA3 achieves the highest negative-to-positive steering success (0.669 versus LLaMA3 baseline 0.417). For positive-to-negative, PREADD-S-OPT scores higher (0.631) but at a disfluency of 68.4 versus ActAdd-OPT 24.2, roughly a 3x fluency penalty. Off-target capability preservation is tested with the ConceptNet LAMA benchmark (wedding steering vector, GPT-2-XL only): P@K for factual recall is negligibly changed across a range of K values, showing that this specific vector on this specific model does not damage general knowledge retrieval.

## Relevance to experiment

ActAdd provides the primary residual-stream causal steering baseline for mechanistic-interpretability sweeps. Three connections make it directly relevant.

First, the layer-sweep result (peak effectiveness at layer 6 for the wedding vector on GPT-2-XL) gives a concrete, falsifiable localization claim for the mechanism program hidden-state candidate inventory: if mechanism activation probes for epistemic-humility-relevant signals localize to the same early-to-middle layer range, that is convergent evidence; if they localize elsewhere, the divergence is informative about whether topic-steering and uncertainty-signaling exploit the same circuit regions.

Second, ActAdd's elicitation-overhang framing motivates the mechanism program hypothesis that base-model internal representations may encode more calibrated uncertainty than the trained output behavior expresses. The paper notes that prompt engineering and finetuning fail to fully elicit model capabilities and that activation engineering can access representations that prompting cannot, which parallels the generation-discrimination gap documented by ITI and the abstention-training question in Phase 1: does SFT or DPO abstention training express or suppress the model's internally represented uncertainty?

Third, the off-target preservation result and the fluency-steering tradeoff together set the ablation safety reference For mechanism work: any activation intervention tested in the mech-interp sweep should be compared against ActAdd's pattern of modest fluency cost at moderate injection coefficients and near-zero factual-recall cost, as a check that capability preservation is maintained.

ActAdd does not report a training before/after calibration or abstention metric, so it does not populate effects.csv directly.
