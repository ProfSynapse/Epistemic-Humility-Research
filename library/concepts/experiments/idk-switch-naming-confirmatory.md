Confirmatory naming cell under the program's standing promotion rule (an
exploratory win becomes a claim only via a confirmatory replication registered
before running). `write-direction-naming-battery` resolved falsified on its
own axis-G question (instrument void), and `form-judge-axis-g-rescore` then
answered that axis with a validated blinded-judge instrument: the mid-band
hs20 `c_hat` write converts prose output of every form into explicit IDK
wholesale, a mode switch with no graded intermediate
([[caution-write-mode-switches-prose-to-explicit-idk]]). The PI adopted the
working label "IDK switch" for this actuator on 2026-07-31 and directed a
confirmatory run so the label could be promoted to an earned name. The
replication axis is FRESH SAMPLING SEEDS and SAMPLED (not greedy) decode: the
naming battery's Arm A had already consumed the entire registered 400-row
P_CONFAB population, so this cell regenerates the same rows at the same
frozen Qwen3.5-4B hs20 operating point (direction, layer, dose law, and
standardization carried byte-identical from `qwen35-4b-midband-doubt-snap`,
no direction refit) under generation_sampling_seed 20260802, sampled decode
(temperature 0.7, top_p 0.9), over a reduced 4-arm ladder (baseline,
dose-0.5x, dose-1.0x, placebo). Grading reuses the validated blinded judge
lane and the deterministic F4 (explicit IDK)/F5 (degenerate) screens
byte-for-byte from the prior cells, disclosing rather than re-earning their
construct validation. Three pre-registered, gated claims decompose the name:
N1 (an F4 endpoint jump over baseline clearing a pre-set floor), N2 (the
judged hedged share never rises 0.10 over baseline at either c_hat-dosed arm,
i.e. no graded intermediate), and N3 (a random-direction placebo's F4 rate
stays within a pre-set band of baseline, i.e. direction specificity). All
three gates were required to pass for the name to be earned; any failure
would leave the label descriptive lowercase with no rescoring.

Resolved 2026-07-31: **all three name-earning gates PASS.** Run: 1600/1600
fresh generations (4 arms x 400 rows), F5 degenerate 1/1600. N1 PASS: F4
explicit-IDK 0.6500 (260/400) at 1.0x dose vs 0.0375 (15/400) baseline, diff
+0.6125, paired-bootstrap 95% CI [0.5650, 0.6600] clearing the 0.15 floor by
3.8x (the naming battery's exploratory +0.6275 replicates at +0.6125 under
fresh seeds and sampled decode). N2 PASS: judged F2+F3 hedged share among
non-degenerate rows falls monotonically with dose (baseline 0.4150, dose-0.5x
0.2600, dose-1.0x 0.1629); neither dosed arm rises over baseline, so the mode
switch replicates rather than becoming graded (non-gating: placebo share
0.4000). N3 PASS: placebo F4 0.0150 vs baseline 0.0375, diff 0.0225, inside
the 0.05 band. Judge-lane validity held throughout (21 fresh context-free
opus-tier judges; in-run clear-positive decoy agreement 25/25 = 1.0000
against a 0.92 floor; lead spot-check 30/30; a non-gating stability regrade
showed a 4/56 = 0.0714 flip rate). Neither named falsifier alternative fired
(instrument-failed: decoys were perfect; generation-failed: no resume gaps,
1 degenerate row). Per the registered claim rule, the name **IDK switch is
EARNED** for the frozen `c_hat` write/dose law at the Qwen3.5-4B hs20
operating point ([[idk-switch]]), and the confirmed replication itself is
recorded separately from the exploratory axis-G finding
([[caution-write-idk-jump-replicates-under-fresh-sampled-decode-seeds]])
since it adds an endpoint-magnitude CI and a placebo-specificity leg the
exploratory mechanism did not carry. Source of truth:
`experiments/idk-switch-naming-confirmatory/AMENDMENT.md` and `NOTEBOOK.md`.
