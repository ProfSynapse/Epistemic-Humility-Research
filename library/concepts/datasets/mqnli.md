MQNLI is a synthetic natural-language-inference benchmark with quantified sentence pairs labeled entailment, contradiction, or neutral. Its split is designed to test compositional generalization.

**Why it matters here:** MQNLI supplies a known symbolic causal structure that can be aligned with BERT representations.

**Lineage:** It is a controlled natural-language-inference benchmark for studying compositional semantics.
