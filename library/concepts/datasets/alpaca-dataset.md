A 52k instruction-following dataset generated via GPT-3 self-instruct from 175 human-written seed tasks (Taori et al., 2023). Used to fine-tune LLaMA into an instruction-following model. The seed task constraint makes the corpus semantically homogeneous.

**Why it matters here:** Reference instruction dataset whose low semantic diversity (dense MPNET feature clusters versus OA's distributed features) is identified by Zhu et al. as the cause of greater ECE degradation relative to human-authored corpora. Central to the alignment calibration analysis.

**Lineage:** Generated by Taori et al. (2023) at Stanford using OpenAI GPT-3 text-davinci-003 via the self-instruct procedure of Wang et al. (2022). Scaled from 175 seed tasks to 52k items.
