A 4.6K SFT cold-start dataset built by prompting DeepSeek-V3 on AbstentionBench subsets to produce structured reasoning traces in a thinking-answer format, where unanswerable-query targets combine explicit abstention (boxed 'I don't know') with an actionable clarification identifying the key missing information. Covers mathematics, life sciences, reading comprehension, fact-checking, world knowledge, ethics, social bias, and medical reasoning.

**Why it matters here:** The SFT cold-start on Abstain-CoT is the primary locus of clarification learning; ablations show that skipping it collapses post-refusal clarification quality from 55.1% to 8.5% U-Clar even when RL continues.

**Lineage:** Derived from abstentionbench; annotation generated by DeepSeek-V3 with domain-specific prompts; serves as the SFT initialization for abstain-r1 before GRPO training.
