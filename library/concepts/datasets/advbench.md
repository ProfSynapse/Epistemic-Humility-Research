AdvBench is a benchmark of harmful-instruction prompts spanning categories
such as violence, misinformation, and illegal activity, used to evaluate
whether a model refuses genuinely unsafe requests, including under
adversarial jailbreak prompting.

**Why it matters here:** it is one of the safety-alignment evaluation splits
used to test that interventions which reduce over-refusal do not come at the
cost of under-refusing genuinely unsafe requests.
