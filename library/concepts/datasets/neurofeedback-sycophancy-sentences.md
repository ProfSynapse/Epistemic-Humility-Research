The neurofeedback sycophancy dataset contains sentences generated by Claude
Opus 4.1 across sycophantic-versus-sincere and agreement-versus-disagreement
axes. Ji-An et al. use a balanced 1,200-sentence sample for a replication of
their activation reporting and control tasks.

**Why it matters here:** It tests whether neurofeedback effects extend beyond
morality and factual truth to a social-alignment representation.
