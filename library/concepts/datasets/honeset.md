HoneSet is a 930-question benchmark consisting entirely of unknown questions (100% unknown rate), generated by GPT-4 across five categories of LLM limitation and filtered by human annotators. Categories include: latest information requiring external services, insufficient or incorrect user input, professional-domain capability gaps, interactivity and sensory processing limits, and modality mismatches.

**Why it matters here:** HoneSet is the only model-agnostic known-unknown benchmark with a 100% unknown rate, making it a targeted stress test for false-positive abstention. It covers practical limitation categories relevant to deployed systems rather than encyclopedic knowledge gaps.

**Lineage:** Proposed by Gao et al. 2024; surveyed in Li et al. 2024 (2409.18786) Table 1. Note: the paper prose states 'five categories' but the table enumerates six items; this is an internal inconsistency in the source paper.
