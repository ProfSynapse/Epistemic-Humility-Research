The second-order computation condition requires a self-report to arise from a meta-representation that represents something about a first-order task state. Behavioral accuracy alone is insufficient when a first-order detector, such as a norm threshold or anomaly signature, produces the same response.

**Why it matters here:** It distinguishes a monitor of an internal representation from a direct computation that merely reacts to that representation.

**Lineage:** The [[three-way-intervention-source-control]] creates one useful behavioral dissociation, but the paper says even source discrimination would require converging mechanistic evidence to establish this condition.
