A privileged stream is a proposed stronger structure than a mere set of accessible representations: a unified, evolving group of [[cognitive-access|cognitively accessible]] representations with shared update and downstream-effect mechanisms. Butlin, Shiller, Plunkett, and Long call the hypothetical true workspace "W-space" and warn that the token-defined [[global-workspace|J-space]] may approximate only part of it.

**Why it matters here:** the distinction protects future J-space experiments from overinterpreting token-based lens coordinates as the full causal write channel. It also gives the actuation program a sharper target: if residual directions are write-inert, the missing object may be the privileged stream rather than every J-lens vector.

**Lineage:** introduced as an interpretive distinction in the Eleos AI Research commentary on the Anthropic workspace paper.
