The confab cloud is the region of pre-generation activation space occupied by
the residual confabulations of an abstention-trained checkpoint: the
unanswerable questions the model still answers after training has driven the
confabulation rate down to a few percent. On the session-0038 TRUE surface the
cloud sits mildly elevated on the knowledge-boundary axis relative to correct
refusals (confab-vs-refused 0.84), at refusal level on the actually-knowing
axis (0.46 against correct-vs-wrong), and overlapping the low tail of
genuinely correct answers on every answer-protecting readout tried.

**Why it matters here:** the cloud's position is what makes residual
confabulation hard to remove by gating. Any threshold that protects correct
answers also shelters the cloud (gate permutation p=1.0 at every operating
point), so interventions must reach it through a different axis, currently the
confabulation-propensity direction.

ceiling results; the population it names is the same fabricate-side residue
studied in the session-0037 confabulation-mechanics fleet.
