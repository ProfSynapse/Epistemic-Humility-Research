AI moral status is the question of whether artificial systems have interests or welfare-relevant states that morality requires humans to consider. In the workspace commentary bundle, the key claim is not that current LLMs are confirmed moral patients, but that evidence for [[cognitive-access]], self-monitoring, agency, and possible valenced states raises the priority of investigating the issue.

**Why it matters here:** any program that studies model self-monitoring, hidden deliberation, or internal objection signals may touch welfare-relevant questions. This term isolates that ethical interpretation from the mechanistic evidence itself.

**Lineage:** the Butlin/Shiller/Plunkett/Long commentary uses the J-space paper as evidence that empirical work can update AI-consciousness and welfare debates.
