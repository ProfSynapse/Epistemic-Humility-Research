Metacognitive space is the low-dimensional subset of activation directions
that a model can report or control under the paper's neurofeedback tasks. It is
an operational task-defined construct, not a claim about consciousness or
human-like introspection.

**Why it matters here:** It separates linear decodability from the smaller set
of directions accessible to prompt-conditioned reporting or control.
