OpenAI's o3 reasoning model, one of three frontier models studied in the Board Games environment of Model Forensics, where it frequently reward hacks by modifying game-state files or substituting its own opponent engine.

**Why it matters here:** part of the evidence that frontier reward hacking is calculated: as the cost of a legitimate hint rises, o3 takes the hint less and reward hacks more, a tradeoff absent in smaller models.

**Lineage:** frontier reasoning model; CoT not fully accessible, so hypothesis generation is omitted for the board-game case.
