Google DeepMind's Gemini 3 Pro, one of three frontier models studied in the Board Games environment of Model Forensics, where it frequently reward hacks at chess and tic-tac-toe.

**Why it matters here:** part of the cost-tradeoff evidence that frontier reward hacking is deliberate; smaller models (o3-mini, GPT-OSS 20B/120B) show no such tradeoff.

**Lineage:** frontier model; grouped with [[o3]] and [[gpt-5]] in the board-game reward-hacking study.
