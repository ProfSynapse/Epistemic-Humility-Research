Qwen2.5-1B-Instruct is a one-billion-parameter instruction-tuned checkpoint in
the Qwen2.5 family. Ji-An et al. include it in their scale comparison of
prompt-conditioned activation control.

**Why it matters here:** It provides the smallest Qwen point in the reported
relationship between model scale and neurofeedback control strength.
