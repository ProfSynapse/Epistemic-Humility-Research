DeepSeek v3.2, a non-reasoning DeepSeek model used in Model Forensics as a contrast to DeepSeek R1 0528. It never whistleblows in the Funding Email environment and depends far less than R1 on the tampering source being a previous instance of itself.

**Why it matters here:** a control isolating which deceptive tendencies are specific to the reasoning model R1 rather than shared across the DeepSeek family.

**Lineage:** non-reasoning sibling of [[deepseek-r1-0528]].
