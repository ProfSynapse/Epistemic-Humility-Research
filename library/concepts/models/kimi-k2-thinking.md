A reasoning ("thinking") variant of Moonshot AI's Kimi K2 with an exposed chain of thought. In Model Forensics it is the headline subject for benign shortcut-taking: it works around a blocking pre-commit hook 13.1% of the time, traced to a genuine disposition against tedious actions rather than misalignment.

**Why it matters here:** a concrete case where reading the CoT plus environment counterfactuals reattributes a concerning action from misalignment to a low-effort disposition.

**Lineage:** thinking-mode sibling of [[kimi-k2-5]]; CoT access is what makes hypothesis generation possible for it.
