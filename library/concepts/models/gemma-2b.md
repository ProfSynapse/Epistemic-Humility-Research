Gemma 2B is Google DeepMind's smaller open-weight member of the original Gemma
family, a decoder-only transformer trained on a large web/code/math text
mixture and released alongside larger Gemma variants in 2024.

**Why it matters here:** Stolfo et al. use Gemma 2B as one of the model
families in which they confirm the presence of entropy neurons, extending the
phenomenon's observed range beyond GPT-2 and Pythia toward the 2-7B scale.
