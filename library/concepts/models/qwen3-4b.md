Qwen3-4B is a four-billion-parameter member of the Qwen3 model family. The
paper includes it in supervised adaptation benchmarks.

**Why it matters here:** It tests whether the post-block intervention results
extend beyond one-billion-parameter models.

**Lineage:** It is a size-specific member of [[qwen3]].
