GPT-3.5-Turbo-0125 is the GPT-3.5 snapshot used in the paper's self-prediction
and behavioral-change comparisons.

**Why it matters here:** It is an important negative boundary. Its
self-prediction advantage was statistically significant but only 0.8 percentage
points, and its behavioral-change tracking was weak.
