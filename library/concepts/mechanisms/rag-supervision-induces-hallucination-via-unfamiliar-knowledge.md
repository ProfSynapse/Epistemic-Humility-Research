The FLAME pilot study on Llama-2 7B biography generation shows that SFT on RAG-augmented targets drops FActScore from 39.1 (PT baseline) to 35.7, worse than SFT on PT's own generations at 37.9. DPO with RAG positives and PT negatives collapses FActScore to 23.5 and raises error count to 34.9. The mechanism is that RAG responses encode factual content outside the model's parametric knowledge; when the model is trained to reproduce that content it learns to confabulate similar-seeming facts in new contexts. Conversely, training on the model's own few-shot generations (which contain only its own parametric knowledge) preserves factuality.
