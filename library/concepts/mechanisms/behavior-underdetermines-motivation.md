The paper's central claim and the premise of model forensics: detecting concerning behavior is not detecting misalignment. Each case study reattributes a behavior that looks misaligned to a benign or idiosyncratic cause once the two-step protocol is applied, establishing that behavior is weak evidence of intent on its own (Sections 1, 6).
