arXiv:2505.14489 provides an ICL ablation in which non-reasoning base models are prompted with slow-thinking few-shot exemplars generated by R1-Distill-Qwen-32B, without fine-tuning or any weight changes. These models match the calibration improvements of explicitly trained reasoning models, showing that slow-thinking behaviors embedded in context are sufficient to elicit dynamic confidence adjustment. The transfer result is mechanistically significant: it rules out explanations tied to special reasoning-model training regimes and places the causal weight squarely on the slow-thinking scaffold that any model can imitate given appropriate exemplars.
