Alpaca's 52k items were generated by expanding 175 seed tasks through GPT-3 self-instruct, producing instruction data with similar format and content per seed. MPNET sentence embedding plus t-SNE visualization (Figure 6 of Zhu et al. 2311.13240) shows Alpaca response features cluster densely while OpenAssistant (OA) features are evenly distributed. Fine-tuning on the clustered Alpaca distribution causes larger ECE increases than fine-tuning on the diverse OA distribution. This implies that data diversity, not just data volume, is a key covariate for calibration outcomes in instruction tuning.
