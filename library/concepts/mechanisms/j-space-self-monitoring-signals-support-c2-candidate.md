The commentary bundle treats hidden J-space content as more than simple reportability: it can contain covert deception, prompt-injection, failure, conflict, or internal objection signals that the model does not voice. Dehaene and Naccache read these as preliminary signs of the self-monitoring criterion C2, while Butlin et al. connect similar signals to possible valence, agency, and moral-status follow-up questions without treating them as decisive.
