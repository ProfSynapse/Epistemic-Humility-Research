Butlin, Shiller, Plunkett, and Long separate three claims: a privileged set of cognitively accessible representations, a unified privileged stream, and a full GWT workspace. They judge the paper strong on the first claim, suggestive on the second, and not yet conclusive on the third because J-space is token-defined, may only approximate a broader W-space, and does not yet establish traditional module-and-broadcast structure.
