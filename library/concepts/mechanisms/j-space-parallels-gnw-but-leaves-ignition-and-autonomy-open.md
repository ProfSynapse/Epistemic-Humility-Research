Dehaene and Naccache read the Anthropic J-space result as a close functional analog of the global neuronal workspace: it is reportable, selective, capacity-limited, globally influential, and recruited for deliberate internal reasoning. Their caveat is that the strongest neuroscience signatures remain only partly tested in Claude: threshold ignition, dual-task competition, dedicated workspace cell populations, autonomous recurrent dynamics, embodied self-state, and persistent episodic memory are missing or still open.
