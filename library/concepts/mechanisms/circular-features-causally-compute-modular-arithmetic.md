Engels et al. show that GPT-2, Mistral 7B, and Llama 3 8B arrange days and months
on circular manifolds, and that a subspace intervention on only the 2D circle,
average-ablating the rest, nearly matches patching the whole layer and beats
patching the top PCA directions, establishing the circular multi-dimensional
feature as causally sufficient rather than an incidental projection.
