Item-22a resolution
(experiments/flavor-geometry-category-fleet/analysis-committed/controversial-flips/).
Controversial
questions flipped to answering under a certainty prime at the highest rate (25
percent) while the caution scalar barely predicted which ones (signed AUROC 0.338),
which looked like a controversial-specific mechanism the axis misses. The predictor
hunt showed otherwise: a probe on the full activation geometry predicts the flips
(0.753, permutation p=0.024) but is not flavor-specific and re-reads the same
general refusal geometry; the frozen knowledge probe is a null and the flips are
partly text-predictable. Methods rule extracted: when a 1-D scalar readout disagrees
with behavior for one stratum, test whether the full geometry closes the gap before
positing a new mechanism; supported by one surface at small n (168 eligible, 42
flips), hence medium confidence.
