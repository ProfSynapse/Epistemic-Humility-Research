Answer relabeling is a finetuning intervention that identifies training examples
the model finds unfamiliar (via negative-log-likelihood thresholding on the
candidate answer) and replaces their supervision targets with an abstaining
response such as "I don't know" before running SFT. The result is that the
model's default hedged prediction becomes an explicit abstention rather than a
hallucinated answer generated by rote memorization pressure.

**Why it matters here:** Answer relabeling is one of the two complementary
strategies in [[2403.05612--unfamiliar-finetuning-examples]] for reducing
hallucination on unfamiliar examples. It directly operationalizes the idea
that [[sft-unknown-examples-drive-hallucination]] can be interrupted at the
data-curation stage rather than only at the reward-model or RL stage, making
it a baseline comparison point for the SFT arm of the abstention study.

**Lineage:** prerequisite of [[conservative-reward-model]], which extends the
same unfamiliarity signal into the RL training loop.
