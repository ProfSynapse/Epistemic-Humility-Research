**Tooling gotcha (2026-07-06):** `exp validate` checks that declared `inputs:`
exist on disk, but gitignored `analysis/` artifacts exist only on the

to `inputs: []` with an explanatory comment. Durable lesson: declare only
committed files in `inputs:`.
