**Tooling gotcha (2026-07-06):** `exp validate` checks that declared `inputs:`
exist on disk, but gitignored `analysis/` artifacts exist only on the
producing worktree. A manifest declaring them PASSES on its own worktree and
BREAKS every clean checkout. AO and AP both had this bug; both were hotfixed
to `inputs: []` with an explanatory comment. Durable lesson: declare only
committed files in `inputs:`.

**Instrument gotcha (2026-07-24), applies to ANY new family:** `use_cache=False`
on a **KV-sharing** model corrupts hidden states from the first shared block
onward while leaving everything below it bit-identical. Found on gemma-4-E4B
(corruption starts hs25; seam at block 24). CPU and GPU extraction agreed at
cos 0.998-0.9998 **because both were wrong the same way** — mutual agreement
across devices is a consistency check, not a correctness check. Durable lesson:
**gate a new family on "does a teacher-forced forward reproduce the model's own
recorded generation," not on cross-device agreement**, and prefer
`use_cache=True` for extraction. See
`docs/sessions/20260724T193620Z-gemma-use-cache-kv-seam-root-cause-and-write-criterion-staging.md`.
